About the property

Description property

Berkeley & Associates are delighted to bring 114 Palmerstown Avenue to the market, an impressive three-bedroom mid-terrace family home with a converted attic, ideally positioned on this highly sought-after residential avenue in Palmerstown. Offering well-proportioned accommodation across three floors, the property provides an excellent combination of space, comfort, and convenience, making it an ideal home for modern family living.

The ground floor comprises a welcoming entrance hall, a comfortable sitting room, a spacious living room, and a bright open-plan kitchen and dining area, along with a conveniently located bathroom.

Upstairs, the property offers three spacious bedrooms and a family bathroom, while the third floor provides a versatile attic conversion, offering valuable additional living or storage space.

Externally, the property benefits from ample off-street parking for 2 cars and a superb south-facing rear garden, which enjoys excellent sunlight throughout the day and provides an ideal outdoor space for relaxation and entertaining. To the rear of the garden is a large garage, offering excellent additional storage and versatility, with the added benefit of rear laneway access.

Overall, 114 Palmerstown Avenue presents an excellent opportunity to acquire a spacious and versatile family home coming to the market in turn key condition also in a highly desirable Palmerstown location.
